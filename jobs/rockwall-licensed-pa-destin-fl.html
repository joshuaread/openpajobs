Rockwall National Public Adjusters, a policyholder-side firm with more than 15 years in the business, is adding a licensed public adjuster in the Destin area on a hybrid, commission-based contractor basis.

The adjuster solicits residential and commercial claims and referral partners, then signs, inspects, documents, and negotiates each loss for the policyholder. An active Florida public adjuster license (or the ability to get one) is required; adjusting, construction, or restoration experience is preferred but not mandatory.

Newer licensees get structured training and mentorship, experienced adjusters get qualified leads and claims systems, and the office handles contracts, compliance, invoicing, and mortgage-company payments. Apply through the BambooHR posting.

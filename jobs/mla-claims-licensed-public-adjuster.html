MLA Claims, a property loss consulting and public adjusting firm headquartered in Lafayette Hill outside Philadelphia, is hiring a licensed public insurance adjuster for a full-time role.

The adjuster handles property claims start to finish: inspecting losses, building estimates and documentation, advising clients, negotiating settlements, and signing both new and in-house leads. It asks for a valid Pennsylvania and/or New Jersey PA license, two or more years of relevant experience, and damage estimating skills (Xactimate a plus).

Pay is salary plus commission based on experience, with healthcare and a 401(k). Apply through the posting or by emailing a resume to the firm.

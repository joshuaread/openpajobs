Swift Public Adjusters wants an outside sales representative to grow its client base across the St. Louis region.

The rep builds referral partnerships, meets with residential and commercial property owners, screens referred claims, and reports on territory activity. A public adjusting license is required, or must be earned within a month of starting.

Pay begins as a $50k–$65k draw against uncapped commission, with full benefits. Swift also lists similar seats in Kansas City and Springfield, MO.

Velocity Public Adjusting (VPA) is hiring Loss Documentation Field Inspectors who travel across the U.S. about 75% of the time to inspect and document property damage for its policyholder clients. No relocation is required.

Inspectors go top to bottom on each property, including roofs, interiors, and structure, and capture detailed photos and written reports from a coverage point of view rather than a contractor's, working with insureds, engineers, and VPA's internal team.

The job is salaried with full benefits and no commission. VPA looks for field adjusting, inspection, or loss documentation experience and says it suits independent adjusters and newer public adjusters. Apply on the posting page.

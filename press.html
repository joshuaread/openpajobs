One-liner

Open PA Jobs is a niche U.S. job board for public adjusters — licensed desks, apprentices, and PA firm roles.

Boilerplate

Open PA Jobs helps public adjusting professionals and firms find each other without the noise of general insurance job boards. Listings link out to employers. The project is independent and not affiliated with NAPIA, FAPIA, or any insurance carrier.

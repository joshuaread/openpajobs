Sponsor Open PA Jobs

Open PA Jobs is a free job board for public adjusters and the PA firms that hire them. Sponsors pay a flat monthly fee for one clearly labeled spot. Two founding options are open.

The board today

The board has 85 live listings from 32 PA firms. These counts come straight from the live listing feed. The site launched in October 2026, so we don't have traffic numbers to share yet, and we won't quote any.

Founding site sponsor: $99/mo

One sponsor only. Your logo, name, and link appear on the homepage and in the footer of every page on the site: guides, state pages, and every job listing. The spot is labeled “Sponsored by”.

Founding page sponsor: $49/mo per page

One sponsor per page. Your logo and link sit in a labeled box near the top of the page. Pages available:

  • Become a Public Adjuster guide, for people working toward a license
  • Remote jobs: remote.html and the remote listings page (23 live listings)
  • Florida: the Florida guide and the Florida listings page (14 live listings)
  • Texas: the Texas guide and the Texas listings page (12 live listings)

Terms

  • Month-to-month. Cancel any month. No contract.
  • Founding price. The founding price stays the same for as long as you keep the spot.
  • Zero-clicks refund. If your spot gets zero clicks in its first month, we refund that month in full.
  • Clicks on your link are counted in Google Analytics, and we'll share the count with you. Sponsored links are marked rel="sponsored".
  • Billing is arranged by email. There is no online checkout for sponsorships.

How to get a spot

Email hello@openpajobs.com with the subject “Sponsor Open PA Jobs”. Tell us which spot you want and send your link and logo. Spots go first come, first served.

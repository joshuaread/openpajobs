Privacy

We keep this simple: your email is for alerts or job-post requests — not for sale.

What we collect

When you subscribe to job alerts or request a founding job post, you submit information through our forms (typically email, optional state focus, and job details for employers).

How forms are processed

Form submissions are sent via Web3Forms, a third-party form delivery service, so we can receive them by email. We do not build or sell marketing lists from this data.

How we use your email

  • Job alerts: to email you when we add openings (if you subscribed)
  • Job posts: to follow up on founding listing requests

We do not sell your email or share it for unrelated advertising.

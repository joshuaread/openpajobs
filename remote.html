Remote & hybrid PA work

Some public adjusting work can be done from a home office; other parts still need boots on the roof. Here’s a practical split — and how to filter this board.

What’s often remote

File review, estimating, carrier follow-up, client status calls, and some sales or intake roles frequently support remote or hybrid schedules. Cradle-to-grave desks with house leads sometimes advertise as remote for licensed PAs who already know the platforms.

What usually stays local or field

Initial inspections, re-inspections, contractor walks, and many storm deployments are location-tied. Even “remote” listings may expect travel for peaks or specific losses. Read each employer’s apply page carefully.

How to filter on Open PA Jobs

On the jobs board, use the search box for keywords like remote, hybrid, or WFH. Combine with role-type filters when you care about licensed vs apprentice tracks. Location text on cards is free-form from employers — search is your friend.

Stay ahead with alerts

Remote openings move quickly. Subscribe to free job alerts and optionally note a state or “remote” focus so new listings reach your inbox.
